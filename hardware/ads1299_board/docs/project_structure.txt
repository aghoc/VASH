KEY PARTS
---------
U1   ADS1299IPAG        8 ch 24-bit AFE, TQFP-64, 0.5 mm pitch
U2   MAX1683EUK+T       2x charge pump, SOT-23-5, 35 kHz, 45 mA
U3   LT1763CS8-5-PBF    low noise LDO, SO-8
J1   2x9 THT terminal   electrode lead wires, 2.54 mm, pad 1.7 / drill 1.0
J4   1x22 socket        ESP32-S3 header J1 side
J5   1x22 socket        ESP32-S3 header J3 side
NT1  NetTie-2           single point AGND / DGND join


POWER CHAIN
-----------
J4.21 (5V from devkit)
  -> C45 10uF -> MAX1683 (C43 3.3uF flying cap)
  -> VBOOST 9.1 V + C44 10uF
  -> R24 10R -> VLDO_IN 8.95 V + C37 10uF      (RC: fc 1.6 kHz, -27 dB @ 35 kHz)
  -> LT1763-5 (BYP C35 10nF, SHDN tied to IN)
  -> +5VA 5.00 V + C36 10uF -> U1 AVDD x6
LDO dissipation (8.95 - 5) x 15 mA = 59 mW

DVDD 3.3 V comes from J4.1 (devkit 3V3).


ESP32-S3 PIN MAP
----------------
ADS1299 *DRDY  (47)  ->  J4.15  ->  GPIO9
ADS1299 *CS    (39)  ->  J4.16  ->  GPIO10   FSPICS0
ADS1299 DIN    (34)  ->  J4.17  ->  GPIO11   FSPID  (MOSI)
ADS1299 SCLK   (40)  ->  J4.18  ->  GPIO12   FSPICLK
ADS1299 DOUT   (43)  ->  J4.19  ->  GPIO13   FSPIQ  (MISO)
ADS1299 START  (38)  ->  J4.20  ->  GPIO14
ADS1299 *RESET (36)  ->  J5.17  ->  GPIO47
ADS1299 *PWDN  (35)  ->  J5.18  ->  GPIO21
GND                  ->  J4.22, J5.21, J5.22

GPIO10-13 are the native FSPI IO_MUX pins, so SPI2_HOST runs without
going through the GPIO matrix. No strapping pins are used.


J1 ELECTRODE TERMINAL  (board seen from the top, X increasing right)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
 X mm     2.18  4.72  7.26  9.80  12.34  14.88  17.42  19.96  22.50
 Y 81.36   1N    2N    3N    4N    BI     5N     6N     7N     8N
 (pin)     17    15    13    11     9      7      5      3      1
 Y 83.90   1P    2P    3P    4P    GN     5P     6P     7P     8P
 (pin)     18    16    14    12    10      8      6      4      2

One column = one differential channel. Put the two leads of a pair into
the top and bottom hole of the SAME column, then twist them from ~2-3 mm
out. BI = active bias electrode. GN = GNDA, scope ground only, not an
electrode. Two 6 x 3 mm slots below the terminal are cable tie anchors.
